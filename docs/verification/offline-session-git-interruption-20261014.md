## Result and semantic boundary

An explicit `retain-unresolved-continuation` decision now retains one supported
unfinished new request through its own flushed receipt. It binds exact accepted
retention/continuation request authority, original correlation and inventory,
the new intent/request bytes, every stage's launch/join or explicit absent-slot
observation, and complete current owned physical state. It preserves both missing
request outcomes and every original/new record and fence. Identical bytes replay
only after fresh checks; changed bytes, distinct IDs, contradictory receipts,
unavailable custody/state and malformed or relabelled authority refuse without
writes. Retained active prelaunch/no-PID slots remain unresolved.

Disposition performs inspection and its own receipt publication only. It cannot
execute Git, resume a command, synthesize success/cancellation or release startup
admission. Actual continuation completion excludes this decision and stays exact.
New mutation admission and public language-composed recovery remain missing.
The [procedure](https://github.com/kw4r3n/nyrwvy/blob/bca45b30053b68abbfd071480e7c062735f410e6/examples/offline-session-physical-git/INTERRUPTION.md)
documents the bounded silent-command/owner and intact-storage contracts. Core,
runtime, backend, saved registry graph and generic storage/task/shield semantics
are unchanged.

## Unproved scope

storage are assumptions. Absent slots rely on the explicitly supplied
`intact-flushed-single-writer-before-slot/v1` contract. Supported new command
captures are silent; other captures remain unresolved. Arbitrary races/descendants,
uncooperative termination, saturated synchronous creation/feeding, cancellation
beyond shields, storage deletion/faults/power loss, multiple writers, full Unix/index
metadata, linked worktrees/live providers, coherently rewritten history,
scale/compaction and peer acknowledgement remain unproved. Independent review
must precede any publication or design of renewed mutation admission.
